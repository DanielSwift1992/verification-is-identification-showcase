# Verification Is Identification · showcase

This package is the demonstration of the notation at scale: a company, its access policy and its intranet site, all declared as Swift types and rendered to pages. It consumes the theory package, [verification-is-identification](https://github.com/DanielSwift1992/verification-is-identification), at a pinned revision and adds no claim to it.

What belongs here: the `Organization` world and its generated roster, `DocumentKit` (pages, tables, SVG, text metrics), the guided walks, the renderers and demos, the generation command, the company reader of the judge with its differential check, and the checks that compare the committed artifacts with the types that produced them.

What does not belong here: the law, the lattice, the papers, the worked constructions of the papers and the canon of verdict fixtures. Those remain in the theory package, and this package depends on them.

Status: skeleton, 18 October 2026. The targets are still in the theory package; the move is tracked as Desk task T-0015 and happens as one complete slice after T-0011 lands.
